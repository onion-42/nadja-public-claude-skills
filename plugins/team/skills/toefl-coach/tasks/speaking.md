# Speaking task bank (written fresh for toefl-coach, not ETS items)

Format facts (confirmed, ETS specs 2026): Speaking = 11 items, ~8 min total:
7 × Listen and Repeat (sentences grow in length and complexity), 4 × Take an Interview.
Each task opens with a short scenario introduction. No preparation time (likely).
Per-question answer time for the Interview: practise with 45 seconds (guess — no ETS figure found).

Audio: the coach speaks each prompt with `say.ps1` so the sentence is HEARD, not read.
Never print a Listen and Repeat sentence before the learner has sent their transcript.

When the bank runs out, write new sets in the SAME shape and append them here.

## Listen and Repeat

Shape: scenario line, then 7 sentences from short (≈5 words) to long (≈20 words, with a relative or
dependent clause). The learner hears each sentence once, repeats it into the phone recorder, and pastes the
transcript, marking stumbles with `*`.

### LR-1 — Scenario: a guide shows new students around the science building.
1. The labs are on the second floor.
2. Please sign in at the front desk.
3. Safety glasses must be worn at all times.
4. The microscope room is open until six on weekdays.
5. If you need a key after hours, ask the security office.
6. Students who have not finished the safety training cannot use the equipment.
7. The seminar room, which was renovated last summer, can be booked through the department website.

### LR-2 — Scenario: a librarian explains the library services.
1. Books can be borrowed for two weeks.
2. The quiet zone is on the top floor.
3. You can renew your loans online.
4. Printers are located next to the main entrance.
5. Group study rooms should be reserved at least a day in advance.
6. Students who return books late will not be able to borrow new ones.
7. The digital archive, which includes journals from the last fifty years, is free for all registered students.

### LR-3 — Scenario: a coordinator welcomes students to a summer research program.
1. Welcome to the summer program.
2. Your badge opens the main building.
3. Lunch is served in the hall at noon.
4. Each team will meet its supervisor on Monday.
5. Please upload your project plan before the end of the first week.
6. Participants who present their results at the final meeting will receive a certificate.
7. The data you collect, unless your supervisor says otherwise, must be stored on the university server.

## Take an Interview

Shape: scenario line, then 4 questions on ONE topic, moving from a simple fact to opinion, explanation,
prediction. 45 seconds per answer (practice value). The coach speaks each question with `say.ps1`,
The learner answers into the recorder, then pastes all 4 transcripts with the time each took.

### TI-1 — Scenario: a researcher is studying how students use their free time.
1. What do you usually do on a free afternoon?
2. Tell me about a hobby you started recently and why you chose it.
3. Some people say university students should spend free time on career-related activities. Do you agree? Why or why not?
4. How do you think the way students spend their free time will change in the next ten years?

### TI-2 — Scenario: the university is collecting opinions about online learning.
1. Have you ever taken a course online? What was it about?
2. Describe one advantage and one difficulty of learning online.
3. Do you think online courses can be as effective as in-person classes? Explain.
4. What should universities do to make online learning better in the future?

### TI-3 — Scenario: a campus magazine is writing about travel and study abroad.
1. Which place you have visited do you remember best?
2. Tell me about a time when something unexpected happened during a trip.
3. Some students think studying abroad is more valuable than studying at home. What is your opinion?
4. How might studying abroad change a student's future career?
