# TOEFL iBT 2026 — scoring rubrics (paraphrased)

Source (confirmed 2026-10-12): ETS Writing Scoring Guide and Speaking Scoring Guide, copyright 2025.
- https://www.ets.org/content/dam/ets-org/pdfs/toefl/writing-rubrics.pdf
- https://www.ets.org/content/dam/ets-org/pdfs/toefl/speaking-rubrics.pdf
- Item points: test specifications 2026, https://www.ets.org/content/dam/ets-org/pdfs/toefl/toefl-ibt-test-specifications-2026.pdf

This is a short paraphrase for coaching, not the ETS text. Open the PDFs for exact wording.

## Points per item (specs 2026)

| Task | Items | Points per item | Scored by |
|---|---|---|---|
| Build a Sentence | 10 | 0 or 1 (right/wrong) | machine |
| Write an Email | 1 | 0–5 | AI engine (+ human raters, likely) |
| Write for an Academic Discussion | 1 | 0–5 | AI engine (+ human raters, likely) |
| Listen and Repeat | 7 | 0–5 | AI engine |
| Take an Interview | 4 | 0–5 | AI engine |

ETS does not publish how item points convert to the 1–6 section band. The coach reports the
**item score 0–5** only and never invents a section band from one drill.

## Write an Email (0–5)

| Score | Short label | What the rater sees |
|---|---|---|
| 5 | fully successful | Elaboration that clearly serves the purpose; varied sentences; precise, idiomatic words; polite, well-organised email (requests, refusals etc. formed correctly); almost no errors beyond normal timed typos |
| 4 | generally successful | Adequate elaboration; some sentence variety; appropriate words; mostly appropriate politeness/register; few errors |
| 3 | partially successful | Task mostly done; elaboration only partly supports the purpose; moderate range; noticeable errors in structure, word forms, idioms or politeness |
| 2 | mostly unsuccessful | Limited or irrelevant elaboration; limited range; errors accumulate; message hard to read |
| 1 | unsuccessful | Very little elaboration; telegraphic phrases; serious frequent errors; mostly copied from the prompt |
| 0 | — | Blank, off-topic, not English, fully copied, random keys |

## Write for an Academic Discussion (0–5)

| Score | Short label | What the rater sees |
|---|---|---|
| 5 | fully successful | Relevant, very clear contribution; well-elaborated explanations, examples, details; varied structures; precise idiomatic words; almost no errors beyond timed typos |
| 4 | generally successful | Relevant contribution, easy to understand; adequate elaboration; some variety; few errors |
| 3 | partially successful | Mostly relevant; part of an explanation/example missing or unclear; some variety; noticeable errors |
| 2 | mostly unsuccessful | Poorly elaborated or partly relevant ideas; limited range; errors accumulate |
| 1 | unsuccessful | Few or no coherent ideas; severely limited range; serious errors; mostly copied |
| 0 | — | Blank, off-topic, not English, fully copied, random keys |

Common timed-writing typos (there/their type) are expected even at score 5.

## Listen and Repeat (0–5)

| Score | What the rater hears |
|---|---|
| 5 | Exact repetition, fully intelligible |
| 4 | Meaning kept, not exact: 1–2 function words missing/changed, OR one content word replaced by a related word, OR tense/number marker wrong, OR two words swapped; self-correction allowed |
| 3 | Essentially full sentence with most content words, but several function words and 1+ content words missing or changed; occasional intelligibility trouble |
| 2 | Large part missing; e.g. only the start and the last few words; fragmentary |
| 1 | Only a few words; mostly unintelligible |
| 0 | No response, no English, unconnected, or only "I don't know" |

## Take an Interview (0–5)

| Score | What the rater hears |
|---|---|
| 5 | Fully answers; on topic and well elaborated; natural pace and pauses; easily intelligible with meaningful rhythm/intonation; accurate range of grammar and vocabulary |
| 4 | Answers, reasonably clear; elaborated but may lack sentence connectors; mostly good pace; occasional words need minor effort; grammar/vocabulary adequate most of the time |
| 3 | Answers with limited elaboration; choppy pace, frequent fillers; some pronunciation/stress problems; limited range restricts precision |
| 2 | Minimally connected to the question; little elaboration or mostly the interviewer's words; often hard to understand |
| 1 | Only vaguely connected; isolated words or phrases; mostly unintelligible |
| 0 | No response, no English, unconnected, or only "I don't know" |

## What the coach can and cannot judge

- **Can judge (from text):** relevance, elaboration, organisation, connectors, grammar, vocabulary range,
  politeness/register, word-level accuracy of a repetition, length vs time.
- **Cannot judge (no audio):** pronunciation, rhythm, intonation, pace, fillers that the transcriber removed.
  For Speaking the coach scores only the text-visible criteria and says so in the feedback line
  `Not scored: pronunciation, pace, intonation`.
- **Transcription caveat:** phone transcribers auto-correct. A "perfect" Listen and Repeat transcript can hide
  a dropped word. The learner marks places where they stumbled with `*` in the transcript; each `*` counts as a missing
  function word in the score.
